                     masked value.
                  ●​ If the third bit (from the right)in the BH register is 1, count the number of
                     0’s in the upper nibble of the BH register. Then multiply the total number
                     of zeroes by 5 and store the product in a variable name Result. (Do not
                     use the MUL keyword.)

Note: Only display the count of 1 and 0 in the console. You can only use add, logical, shift and
rotate instructions to solve this.

 Sample value      Sample final value     Explanation
 BH = 193d         Result : Not           BH = 11000001b (3rd bit from right = 0)
                   applicable here        Masked value = 001111110b

                                          No. of 0’s in the upper nibble = 2
                                          Result = 2 * 5 = 10d = 0AH
